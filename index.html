About Me

My name is John Hui. My major is currently undecided, but I am heavily considering computer science or game development. I am taking this course due to my interest in said major and am hoping to learn a lot about coding to be able to create my own projects.

My first memorable experience with AI was around 10th grade when I used it to help me with school work. When ChatGPT first launched back in 2022, I didn't pay it much attention. Then, after about two years when it became more advanced, I gave it a test run, and it felt like an invention right out of a sci-fi movie. ChatGPT helped me immensely with things I struggled with, including paragraph structure, research, tedious tasks, and math equations.

However, that is not to say I'm all for AI without reservation. While AI is helpful with many tasks, there are certainly wrong ways to use it—ways that will definitely cause harm in the long run, especially over-relaying on it for the simplest things. I am most curious about outcome 5: evaluating AI and large language models at a conceptual level.

Outside of academics, I enjoy playing video games, weightlifting, playing basketball, and American football in my free time.
